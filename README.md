## Privacy Policy for Echo App

Echo App is a social media platform designed for sharing secrets, confessions, and thoughts with complete anonymity. We take your privacy seriously and are committed to protecting your identity and data. Below is our privacy policy outlining how we handle information on Echo App.

### 1. Anonymity

- **No Personal Information Required:** You are not required to provide any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, or phone number) to use Echo App.
- **Anonymous Posting:** All posts, confessions, and secrets shared on Echo App are published without any link to your real identity.

### 2. Data Collection

- **Minimal Data Storage:** We only collect the information necessary to operate the app, such as anonymous posts and non-identifying metadata (e.g., timestamp, device type).
- **No Tracking:** We do not track your location, browsing history, or use tracking cookies.

### 3. Data Usage

- **Purpose:** Collected data is solely used to maintain the app's functionality, improve user experience, and ensure community safety (e.g., filtering harmful content).
- **No Sharing:** We do not share, sell, or trade any user data with third parties.

### 4. Security

- **Encryption:** All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted.
- **Access Control:** Only authorized personnel have limited access to the backend for maintenance and moderation purposes.

### 5. User Rights

- **Delete Your Data:** You can request deletion of your posts at any time. Since no personal identifier is stored, deletion requests are handled based on unique post IDs.
- **No Profile:** There are no user profiles or accounts, further protecting your anonymity.

### 6. Community Guidelines

- **Respect & Safety:** We are committed to keeping Echo App a safe, respectful space. Posts containing hate speech, threats, or illegal content will be removed.
- **Reporting:** Users can flag content that violates community guidelines for review.

### 7. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy as Echo App evolves. Changes will be reflected on this page, with the date of revision.

---

**If you have any questions or concerns about privacy on Echo App, please contact the repository owner.**
